## Best Practices

- **Length:** All primary subject lines are under 50 characters for mobile optimization
- **Tone:** Casual, conversational, on-brand with Bobby Seamoss voice
- **Urgency:** Used strategically on time-sensitive campaigns (Dec 20, Jan 15)
- **Personalization:** First name personalization can increase open rates by 10-20%
- **Emojis:** Use sparingly; test with your audience before committing
- **Preview Text:** Complements subject line without repeating it
